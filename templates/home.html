How to Open an Account

Prime Reserve Bank Authorized Onboarding Process

Enhanced Security & KYC Verification:
In strict compliance with banking regulations, customer accounts are officially onboarded by Bank Managers at authorized branch counters to prevent identity fraud.
Simple 4-Step Account Onboarding:
1. Visit Nearest Branch

Visit your nearest branch with your official identity documents (PAN Card / Aadhaar Card).

2. Manager KYC Clearance

Bank Manager verifies your identity and issues your unique 11-digit CIF and 14-digit Account Number.

3. Instant Email Welcome Pack

Your NetBanking User ID and Temporary Password are automatically dispatched to your personal email.

4. First-Time Setup & UPI PIN

Sign in via NetBanking Login, verify with Email OTP, and generate your 6-digit secure UPI PIN for 24x7 transfers.
